openGym

Your workouts. Your weights. Your data.

A free, open-source gym & body-weight tracker. No subscription, username/password login, no telemetry — ever.

Everything a training log needs

Built in the gym, not in a boardroom

Every feature exists because it was needed on the gym floor — nothing is there to sell you anything.

▶️

Guided workouts

Knows what day it is, starts today's session, pre-fills your weights from last time, times your rest, spots PRs.

🏋️

1,324 exercises

Searchable library with animated demos, filterable by body part and the equipment you own — plus your own custom exercises.

🗓️

Weekly plan

A routine per weekday, and any day can be rescheduled when life happens — without touching the plan.

⚖️

Body weight & goal

Interactive chart with your goal line; every change is colored by whether it moves toward it.

💪

Muscle map

A body diagram shaded by how much work each muscle got — and which ones got nothing.

🟩

Activity heatmap

A year view of your training time. Streaks included.

📈

Progression rules

Linear, Greyskull LP, double progression or added time — per routine, overridable per exercise. Your weights are already right when the session opens, and every target tells you why.

⏱️

Timed sets

Planks, hangs and carries are logged by time, with a timer for the set itself. Stop early and it logs what you actually held.

🧮

Estimated 1RM

Per exercise, from your best eligible set — and it names which one. Won't guess above 12 reps.

🔗

Supersets & cardio

Pair exercises back-to-back with one rest, and log time + speed where weight × reps makes no sense.

📤

Share a plan

Send someone your routines as a small file — no workouts, no weigh-ins — or print it as a clean PDF. Importing merges, never overwrites.

🎯

Effort per set

Rate a set as RIR or RPE — whichever scale you already think in. Optional, and every set keeps the scale it was logged with.

☀️

Screen stays awake

No unlocking the phone between sets. The screen stays lit while a workout runs, and lets go the moment you finish.

📥

Bring your history

Import from FitNotes, Strong, Hevy — including the RPE they recorded — or body weight straight out of an Apple Health export.

🌍

12 languages

Full UI translation, exercise instructions in 10 languages, loaded on demand.

🔐

Username/password login

Self-hosted profiles sign in with username/password. Passkeys optional.

🎨

Designed, not assembled

Light & dark themes, 8 accent colors, a hand-drawn icon set. Looks the same on every phone.

📦

Yours to keep

One-tap JSON export and import. Your training log is a file you own, not a row in someone's cloud.

Get openGym

One app, two flavors

Same codebase, your choice of where the data lives: entirely on your phone, or on a server you control.

📱 Mobile app

For anyone — install and you're done.

  • Everything stays on your phone (offline-first)
  • No account, no server, no setup required
  • Native workout-day reminders
  • Backups via the share sheet
  • Optional: sign in to sync with your self-hosted server
